04 • WORKSHOP EXPERIENCE

Your Workshop Experience

Why this session is built differently, what you’ll actually do at your workstation, and the tangible skills and project assets you’ll take home.

PART 1 • THE APPROACH

Why This Workshop is Useful

Most AI sessions are passive slide presentations. This workshop is structured around hands-on experimentation, live trials, and direct student relevance.

01 • HANDS-ON LEARNING

Active trial over passive lectures

Skip theoretical monologues. You’ll run real prompts directly at your workstation, test model behavior, and troubleshoot responses live.

02 • GENUINE STUDENT VALUE

Grounded in daily student reality

Focus on what genuinely accelerates college work: structuring research, breaking down complex topics, debugging errors, and project prep.

03 • CROSS-DISCIPLINE BUILD

Collaborate across departments

Connect with students across different branches, take part in collaborative exercises, and build interdisciplinary perspectives.

PART 2 • THE 4-STAGE SESSION JOURNEY

What You’ll Do During the Session

A clear, fast-paced progression from your first test prompt to pitching a completed concept.

01
EXPLORATION

Discover & Demystify

Get an honest, hype-free orientation to Google Gemini capabilities, model reasoning, and prompt logic.

02
HANDS-ON TRIAL

Experiment Live

Test structured prompt frameworks at your workstation with real-time facilitator and peer feedback.

03
CREATIVE BUILD

Build a Concept

Pair Gemini prompt reasoning with creative visual tools to develop an original concept deck and mockups.

04
SHOWCASE

Pitch & Celebrate

Present your creation in an energetic 60-second lightning round and see what other teams built.

PART 3 • OUTCOMES & SKILLS

What You Can Take Away

Concrete capabilities, reusable templates, and verified credentials you keep long after the session concludes.

Reusable Prompt Frameworks

Take home battle-tested prompt templates engineered for academic research, assignment drafting, complex synthesis, and exam prep.

Multimodal Visual Creation

Experience pairing prompt reasoning with visual generation to produce moodboards, concept assets, and presentation slides rapidly.

A Complete Project Prototype

Walk away with a tangible concept deck and visual asset package you built yourself during the session—ready for your portfolio.

Verified Certificate & Award Eligibility

Confirm your attendance for official certificate recognition, and qualify for conditional workshop awards and prizes.
